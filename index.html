Methodology Walkthrough

First Principle:

I don't ask: "What tool should we teach?"
I ask: "What should this learner be able to think, create, or solve by the end?"

The AI Mindset:

Think → Ask → Build → Test → Debug → Improve. AI amplifies thinking; it doesn't replace student problem-solving.

6-Step Learning Design Cycle
1. Understand Prior context & goals
2. Experience Story or real problem
3. Explore Test ideas & test code
4. Build Tangible application
5. Debug Isolate & troubleshoot
6. Share Reflect & iterate

Inclusive Technology Education

Enlarge
Making Technology Learning Accessible to Every Learner

I believe every learner can explore and understand technology when given the right support, time and learning environment. My approach begins with understanding what a student can do, how they learn best, and where they may need more guidance.

Learning at Their Own Pace

Breaking concepts into smaller, manageable steps and giving learners enough time to understand, practise and build confidence without feeling overwhelmed.

Learning Through Exploration

Using visual coding, hands-on activities and practical projects to make technology easier to understand. I encourage trial and error, allowing students to experiment, make mistakes and try again with patience and guidance.

“Inclusive education begins with understanding the learner, building on what they can do, and giving them the time, support and confidence to discover what they can do next.”

Featured Research

Bandwidth Utilization and Efficiency in MACA and MACA-BI: A Study

This research presents a comparative study of the sender-initiated MACA (Multiple Access Collision Avoidance) and the receiver-initiated MACA-BI (Multiple Access Collision Avoidance By Invitation) protocols in Mobile Ad hoc Networks (MANETs). It evaluates their channel access mechanisms, bandwidth utilization, and efficiency, demonstrating how MACA-BI simplifies handshakes to a two-way RTR-DATA exchange to cut turnaround overhead, minimize packet collisions, and offer greater robustness against transmission failures

Authors: Dependra Dhakal, Saroj Bhujel, Priyadarshini Basu, and Biswaraj Sen Published In: International Journal of Computer Applications & Information Technology (IJCAIT), Vol. I, Issue II, September 2012 (ISSN: 2278-7720)
